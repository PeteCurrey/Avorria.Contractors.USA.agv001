# Avorria — Initial SEO & Programmatic Content Strategy Roadmap

This document defines the 7-tier organic search acquisition roadmap for Avorria in the US contractor market. The architecture is engineered around high search intent, original value, structured data governance, and natural conversion into the free tool, account creation, and paid Contractor Passport verification.

---

## The Non-Negotiable Content Quality Framework

Every page generated or published by Avorria must answer:
1. **What is the contractor searching for?** (Exact intent, problem, or regulatory mandate)
2. **What do they actually need?** (A workable document, calculation, or clear regulatory checklist)
3. **Can we give them a direct answer?** (No 1,500-word fluff intros; immediate structured guidance)
4. **Can we provide an interactive tool or downloadable template?** (Instant utility)
5. **Is there a natural reason to create an account?** (Cloud auto-save, PDF branding, COI expiration alerts)
6. **Is the page materially different from related pages?** (No spun thin variations)

## The 7 Priority SEO Tiers

### Priority 1 — Commercial Intent (Highest Purchase & Pre-qualification Intent)
Targets project leaders, commercial contractors, and business owners looking for credibility platforms and pre-qualification solutions.

* `/platform` — Core platform proposition (Create, Comply, Prove, Win).
* `/contractor-compliance` — Central compliance tracking and COI management hub.
* `/contractor-verification` — Evidence-based contractor credential verification program.
* `/contractor-passport` — Shareable verified contractor profile pack.
* `/win-work` — Commercial bid preparation, proposals, and pre-qualification packs.
* `/pricing` — Transparent plans (Free Starter, Professional, Verified, Business).

### Priority 2 — Interactive Tool Intent (High Conversion Generators)
Contractors actively seeking digital generators to solve immediate field problems.

* `/tools/job-hazard-analysis-jha-generator` — Interactive task hazard and OSHA control builder.
* `/tools/contractor-quote-calculator` — Labor burden, overhead, and margin calculator.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/tools/toolbox-talk-agenda-builder` — Weekly safety meeting topic generator.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/tools/certificate-of-insurance-audit-tool` — Instant COI expiration and limit checker.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/tools/contractor-readiness-calculator` — Free 10-point credibility assessment.

### Priority 3 — Template Intent (High Volume Organic Capture)
Contractors searching for standardized forms required by general contractors and job site safety officers.

* `/templates/job-hazard-analysis-jha` — Standard task hazard analysis form.
* `/templates/job-safety-analysis-jsa` — 3-column safety procedure breakdown.
* `/templates/construction-safety-plan` — Site-specific Health and Safety Plan (HASP).
* `/templates/toolbox-talk` — Weekly safety meeting roster and log.
* `/templates/contractor-proposal` — Commercial proposal, scope of work, and terms.
* `/templates/change-order` — Contract modification and scope addition agreement.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/templates/daily-construction-report` — Field activity and weather log.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/templates/subcontractor-agreement` — Master subcontract terms.

### Priority 4 — Compliance & Regulatory Intent (Research & Risk Mitigation)
Contractors and safety directors researching statutory mandates, insurance requirements, and OSHA standards.

* `/guides/contractor-compliance-checklist` — Complete 30-point US contractor compliance checklist.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/guides/osha-competent-person-requirements` — Designation guidelines.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/guides/contractor-insurance-minimums-commercial` — GL, WC, Auto, and Umbrella thresholds.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/guides/how-to-manage-subcontractor-cois` — Prequalification workflow.

### Priority 5 — Trade-Specific Foundations (High Topical Authority)
Tailored technical compliance, hazard controls, and documentation for individual trades.

* `/industries/electrical-contractor-compliance` — NFPA 70E, arc flash, and LOTO safety standards.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/industries/hvac-contractor-compliance` — EPA Section 608, refrigerant handling, and confined spaces.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/industries/roofing-contractor-compliance` — OSHA 1926 Subpart M fall protection plans.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/industries/plumbing-contractor-compliance` — Trenching, shoring, and hazardous materials.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/industries/general-contractor-safety-program` — Multi-employer job site management.

### Priority 6 — Geographic & State Jurisdiction Hubs
State licensing boards, local insurance rules, and state-specific safety plans.

* `/states/texas-contractor-requirements` — TDLR licensing, Texas municipal rules, non-subscriber WC rules.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/states/california-contractor-requirements` — CSLB licensing, Cal/OSHA IIPP requirements.
* *(Roadmap Phase 2)* `/states/florida-contractor-requirements` — DBPR licensing and hurricane contingency safety.

### Priority 7 — Trade + Document Matrix (Programmatic Scale)
Only executed where genuine search demand exists with unique, trade-tailored content seeds:

* Electrical Contractor JHA
* HVAC Contractor Safety Plan
* Roofing Fall Protection JHA
* Plumbing Trenching Safety Plan
